# Corda Campus world: sources, attribution and approximations

Generated on 2026-10-24. This is a lightweight simulation reconstruction, not a survey or a flight-planning map. Local axes are metres East/North/Up. The world origin is 50.951737 N, 5.352412 E; flat ground is assumed to be 50 m AMSL, preserving the earlier environment origin. This is not a validated conversion of Belgian TAW heights to WGS84 ellipsoid height.

## Data and attribution

**Bron: Grootschalig Referentie Bestand Vlaanderen, Digitaal Vlaanderen.** Retain this attribution with the source snapshots and generated building geometry. The environment's MIT licence does not replace the source-data attribution requirements.

- Current building polygons: [GRB](https://www.vlaanderen.be/digitaal-vlaanderen/onze-diensten-en-platformen/basiskaart-vlaanderen-grb), official WFS `https://geo.api.vlaanderen.be/GRB/wfs`, feature type `GRB:GBG`. `data/buildings.geojson` contains 82 footprints >=12 m² intersecting the local rectangle [-246,-132,404,388] m. Boundary buildings are kept whole. Native **EPSG:3812 (Belgian Lambert 2008)** coordinates are transformed locally with PROJ; source dates and stable OIDNs remain in the snapshot. The WFS CRS84 response was found about 74 m west / 28 m south of the same native-grid features. The native grid agrees with independently requested EPSG:3857 features and the official orthophoto. Do not use that service's CRS84 transformation or compensate with a guessed translation.
- Building heights: [3D GRB](https://www.vlaanderen.be/digitaal-vlaanderen/onze-diensten-en-platformen/basiskaart-vlaanderen-grb/3d-grb), distributed by the provincial [GRB service, layer 6](https://geo.limburg.be/arcgis/rest/services/GRB/MapServer/6). `data/heights.json` retains only height attributes linked by **exact GRB OIDN**, with HN_P99, H_KWAL and source dates. HN_P99 is a relative building-height estimate, not an absolute terrain elevation. The old height dataset misses newer buildings. The earlier comparison against the WFS CRS84 response showed a systematic positional difference; the native-grid import above resolves the footprint datum issue. Provincial height geometry is not needed and is not shifted into the world. No spatial-overlap height transfer is performed.
- Corda 1 identification and visual roof direction: official [campus visitor map](https://www.cordacampus.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Visitor-Map_2K.png) and [Corda 1 photograph](https://www.cordacampus.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Corda-building-1.jpg). These images were consulted as references, and are not bundled in the runtime assets.
- Corda 1 maximum height and sloped form: project architect [eld](https://eld.be/projects/corda-campus/) states seven floors and a maximum height of 30 m. The model uses that maximum, with an **approximated** planar roof falling to a 4 m low edge along its footprint's long axis. This is not roof survey/BIM geometry. The companion bar wedge and Corda 2/3/4 heights are visual estimates, explicitly listed in `site.json`.
- Corda 2 and 3 sloping forms: architect [eld describes phases 2/3](https://www.eld.be/next-phases-corda-campus/) as volumes similar to Corda 1; the official visitor map shows the falling roofs. Both are modelled as long-axis wedges, high at the southern end, with **estimated** 18 m high and 4 m low edges. These dimensions and roof angles are visual approximations, not measured heights.
- Corda Arena maximum height: campus [Masterplan 2.0 brochure](https://www.cordacampus.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Masterplan-2.0-brochure-en.pdf), 24 m. Current GRB provides its exterior footprint. The [official 2024 exterior render](https://www.cordacampus.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Corda-Arena-Render-1.jpg) shows a pale upper volume, a glazed ground-level entrance and projecting cube frames/panels. The [venue reference](https://www.corda-arena.com/venue) shows the enclosed hall and gallery levels. These guide a **schematic hollow hall**, thin collision walls, full roof, 4.2 m glazing strip and simplified cube ornament. The survey footprint's inner ring is interpreted as a construction-stage hall void, not a finished outdoor courtyard. An 8 m wide, 3.625 m high entrance is held open as a simulation aid; it is not a surveyed doorway dimension or an assertion about current access. Glazing retains collision despite visual transparency. Interior galleries, thicknesses and decorative panels are approximations; stairs, seating, rooms and detailed services are omitted. The 2022 ZJA competition entry is explicitly marked unbuilt and is not the model reference.
- Water tower: the `GRB:KNW` feature **OIDN 9709 / KNW.36480** is manually identified at the tower location against the official orthophoto and visitor map; its original label is “hoogspanningsmast / openbare TV mast”, not “water tower”, so that classification is retained in `data/landmarks.geojson`. The native footprint fixes the position (approximately 50.9519476 N, 5.3538341 E). A roughly 35 m tower with four open lattice supports, cross-braces, spiral stairs and a saucer tank is approximated from the visitor map and campus references; [local reporting](https://www.tvl.be/nieuws/hogere-hekken-moeten-klimmers-van-philipswatertoren-houden-183410) corroborates the approximate overall height. Member sizes, tank profile and stair dimensions are estimates, not engineering drawings. The adjacent hut remains its mapped GRB building.
- Parking: outlines manually traced from the official [latest winter orthophoto service](https://www.vlaanderen.be/datavindplaats/catalogus/orthofotomozaiek-middenschalig-winteropnamen-kleur-meest-recent-vlaanderen), `https://geo.api.vlaanderen.be/OMWRGBMRVL/wms`, layer `Ortho`, and checked against the [2024 mobility map](https://www.cordacampus.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Mobility-Map_2K.png). **Bron: Digitaal Vlaanderen, Orthofotomozaïek, middenschalig, winteropnamen, kleur, meest recent.** Six simplified paved areas use illustrative 2.6×5 m bays and 6 m aisles, clipped away from mapped buildings; counts are generated simulation bays, not real capacity. This is a mixed-date reference scene, not a current parking/access map: the campus announced visitor-parking changes in 2026. Reference images are not redistributed or used as textures. Exact WMS extent/request is in `data/reference-requests.json`.
- GUI layout: based on `gui/gui.config` from [Gazebo Sim 8](https://github.com/gazebosim/gz-sim/blob/gz-sim8/src/gui/gui.config), Apache-2.0. `gui.config.in` changes the initial camera, disables the quick-start dialog, and starts unpaused. ## Geometry and height rules

The offline generator preserves concave outlines and outdoor courtyards, simplifies edges by at most 0.12 m and produces watertight building triangle meshes with matching collision and visual geometry. The Arena uses a separate roofed architectural shell instead of the construction-stage extrusion; parking markings are visual-only triangle surfaces over the colliding ground. Curved outlines remain polygonal approximations, not bounding boxes. Roof sections are mostly flat; Corda 1, its companion, Corda 2 and Corda 3 are wedges. Corda 1/2/3 use separate green roof surfaces and approximate facade colours (blue-grey glass, light cladding, warm grey brick respectively), based on the visitor map. Colours are simplified solid materials; facade patterns and glazing details are omitted. Corda 4 and 5 have estimated pale facade colours and schematic horizontal window bands. Other detailed facades, trees, vehicles, road networks, terrain detail and interiors outside the Arena are not reconstructed.

Heights come from an explicit architectural override, then an exact-ID positive HN_P99, then an estimate (6 m for footprints <250 m²; otherwise 12 m). Every generated building's height source is listed in `simulation/campus.json`. The mesh has one height per footprint except the four wedge roofs; buildings with real stepped roofs may need future manually authored subdivisions.

The generated launch is **on the actual Corda 1 footprint** at local (-11,-10) m, approximately 50.9516471 N, 5.3522555 E. Correcting the WFS datum conversion moves both the buildings and their launch position into alignment with the official orthophoto; the world origin stays unchanged. A **synthetic 4×4 m level launch platform** sits above the sloping roof, with an H marking and visual supports. This is a simulation aid, not a real helipad. Generated roof/platform/spawn elevations are in `simulation/campus.json`; the drone should settle around 23.9 m above the assumed campus ground.


## Regeneration

Runtime simulation uses the checked-in meshes; it needs no GIS libraries or internet. Changes to outlines/heights require regenerating assets and rebuilding the ROS package. `site.json`, the native-grid snapshots and `gui.config.in` are authoritative inputs. The generated report records input hashes and provenance.

Run the generator in Docker with an isolated GIS venv (does not replace the ROS Python environment):

```bash
# Inside the development container, with this repository mounted at /work:
python3 -m venv --system-site-packages /tmp/campus-gis
/tmp/campus-gis/bin/pip install -r /work/tools/campus/requirements.txt
/tmp/campus-gis/bin/python /work/tools/campus/generate.py
build_ros2_workspace --packages-select px4_dev_examples
```

For a source-data refresh, use `fetch.py` in the same venv. It downloads to a separate destination for review, never overwrites the checked-in snapshot automatically. Keep older snapshots until geometry, building IDs, data provenance and the launch roof have been revalidated. The provincial server currently omits an intermediate certificate; the fetcher can load the official GlobalSign intermediate into a request-local trust context, leaving normal TLS verification enabled.

Offline geometry regression checks (run in the same GIS venv): `python tests/campus_geometry.py`. They cover the native-grid control point, landmark position, open Arena entrance, colliding glazing/roof and mesh validity.
